# The Lion and the Mouse

Premise: a tiny mouse keeps a big promise to the forest king.

- We see: the lion's chest rising, dust in a sunbeam, a small mouse peeking from the grass.
- Narration: "In the deep forest slept Lion, the king, tired from a long day."
- Dialogue: none.
- Sound: soft wind, distant birds, one warm music note.

- Dialogue: none.

- Narration: "Lion woke and caught Mouse under one soft paw."

- Narration: "Lion laughed kindly and let her go."

- Narration: "Days later hunters' ropes caught the king, and his roar filled the trees."
- Dialogue: none.
- Sound: rope creak, low roar, tense strings.

### Scene 6 (0:34-0:41, 7s)

- Narration: "Mouse heard the roar and remembered her promise."
- Dialogue: Mouse: "Hold still, King. I am coming."
- Sound: running patter, heartbeat rhythm.

### Scene 7 (0:41-0:49, 8s)
- Shot: extreme close then pull-back. The mouse gnaws rope threads one by one.

- Narration: "With small teeth she cut the ropes, thread by thread, until the king stood free."
- Dialogue: Lion: "Thank you, little friend."
- Sound: gentle gnawing rhythm, rope twang, rising warm music.

### Scene 8 (0:49-0:57, 8s)

- We see: big paw and small footprints side by side, fireflies rising, calm trees.
- Narration: "So the smallest friend can keep the biggest promise."
- Dialogue: none.
- Sound: full warm theme, fireflies shimmer, soft end.

Total duration: 57 seconds.
